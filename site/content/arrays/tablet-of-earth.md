## Characteristics

The Earth array is associated with:
- **Solid positioning** — strong pawn structures and defensive lines
- **Material focus** — emphasis on piece value and exchanges
- **Stability** — slow but steady advancement
- **Balanced tempo** — strictly alternating control between players

## Turn Order: Counter-Clockwise Alternation

The Tablet of Earth begins with **Yellow** (Team Earth) and strictly alternates between teams:

1. <span class="army-badge army-badge--yellow">Yellow</span> (Player One)
2. <span class="army-badge army-badge--blue">Blue</span> (Player Two)
3. <span class="army-badge army-badge--red">Red</span> (Player One)
4. <span class="army-badge army-badge--black">Black</span> (Player Two)

This creates the most balanced rhythm — every move is answered by an opponent. No team ever gets consecutive actions, rewarding solid, methodical play over explosive combinations.

## Strategic Implications

The Earth configuration rewards patient, positional play:

- **Opening**: Develop methodically — your opponent will always respond before your ally moves
- **Midgame**: Maintain solid structures — no consecutive moves means no surprise combinations
- **Endgame**: Grind advantages — material matters more when tempo is always balanced
