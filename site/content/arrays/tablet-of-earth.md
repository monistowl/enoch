## Characteristics

The Earth array is associated with:
- **Solid positioning** — strong pawn structures and defensive lines
- **Material focus** — emphasis on piece value and exchanges
- **Stability** — slow but steady advancement

## Turn Order Note

The Tablet of Earth begins with **Yellow** (Team Earth), creating an alternating pattern:

1. Yellow (Team Earth)
2. Blue (Team Air)
3. Red (Team Earth)
4. Black (Team Air)

This interleaved order means each move is followed by an opponent's move, creating a more balanced tempo.
