## Characteristics

The Air array is associated with:
- **Swift attacks** — aggressive opening possibilities
- **Intellectual play** — complex tactics and combinations
- **Mobility** — emphasis on piece activity over material

## Turn Order Note

The Tablet of Air begins with **Red** (Team Earth), giving that team first-move advantage:

1. Red (Team Earth)
2. Yellow (Team Earth)
3. Black (Team Air)
4. Blue (Team Air)

This reversed order creates opportunities for Team Earth to establish initiative early.
