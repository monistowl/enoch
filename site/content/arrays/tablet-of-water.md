## Characteristics

The Water array is associated with:
- **Defensive play** — pieces positioned for protection
- **Patience** — slower development favoring long-term strategy
- **Adaptability** — fluid response to opponent threats

## Turn Order Note

Unlike the Tablet of Fire's clockwise order (Blue → Red → Black → Yellow), the Tablet of Water uses a **modified sequence** where allies move consecutively:

1. Blue (Team Air)
2. Black (Team Air)
3. Yellow (Team Earth)
4. Red (Team Earth)

This creates a different tactical rhythm where each team gets two consecutive moves.
