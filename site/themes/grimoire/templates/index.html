The Four Armies

Enochian Chess is played by four players in two teams. Team Air (Blue and Black) faces Team Earth (Red and Yellow) across an 8×8 board. Unlike standard chess, kings can be captured—and when they fall, their entire army freezes in place.

Origins

Enochian Chess emerged from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn in the late 19th century. Documented by S.L. MacGregor Mathers and later expanded by Chris Zalewski, it was designed as both a game of strategy and a tool for divination.

Today, it stands as one of the most intricate chess variants ever devised—a game where four armies clash, alliances matter, and fallen kings leave frozen soldiers behind.
