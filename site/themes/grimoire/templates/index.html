Quick Rules

Teams

  • Air: Blue + Black
  • Earth: Red + Yellow

Turn Order

Blue → Red → Black → Yellow, repeat

Victory

Checkmate both enemy kings to win for your team.

Pieces

  • King - Moves 1 square any direction
  • Queen - Rook + Bishop combined
  • Rook - Straight lines (ranks/files)
  • Bishop - Diagonal lines
  • Knight - L-shape, jumps pieces
  • Pawn - Forward 1, captures diagonally

Special Rules

  • Frozen Army: When a king is checkmated, that army freezes
  • Throne Overlay: Pieces can sit on allied king thrones
  • No Castling and No En Passant

How to Play

  • Click a piece to see legal moves
  • Green dots = moves, Red dots = captures
  • Click a destination to move
